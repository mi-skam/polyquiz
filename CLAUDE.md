## Domain Background for the application

### Relationship Models and Types Discussed
While "The Ethical Slut" doesn't present a strict taxonomy of relationship models, it covers various forms of ethical non-monogamy:
#### Primary Relationship Types Covered:
1. Open Relationships
The book discusses open relationships as arrangements that allow sexual relationships with others outside the primary partnership The Ethical Slut - Wikipedia
2. Swinging
Swinging is presented as couples engaging in sexual activities with other couples or individuals, typically in social or group settings, with a focus on recreational sexual exploration rather than emotional connections WikipediaThe Monogamy Experiment
3. Polyamory (Multiple Forms)
The book acknowledges different approaches to polyamory:

Hierarchical Polyamory: Relationships structured with varying degrees of importance, where "people who live in a marriage-like arrangement are primaries, the people they love but don't live with are secondaries, the people they enjoy spending (often sexual) time with, but often with less commitment, are tertiaries" MediumMedium
Non-hierarchical Polyamory: Approaches that "respect the inter-dynamics and affectional roles of each relationship within the polycule without a ranking system" What are Polyamory and Ethical Non-monogamy?

4. Group Marriage/Polyfidelity
The book touches on "polyfidelity" where "all partners in a group agree not to have romantic and sexual relationships outside the established group" All About Ethical Non-Monogamy
#### Contemporary Models (Added in Later Editions):
The third edition (2017) expanded to include:
5. Solo Polyamory
A form where "an individual chooses to be their own 'primary partner,' building connections without the assumption of progressing up the 'relationship escalator'" PolyamoryukDarcy Magazine
6. Kitchen Table Polyamory
A style emphasizing "family vibes in the polycule" where "all members get together for family gatherings and provide communal support" WikipediaDarcy Magazine
7. Parallel Polyamory
A style where "relationships a person has are largely independent of one another, and there may be little or no contact or relationship between a person's various partners" More Than TwoPolyamoryuk
8. Relationship Anarchy
Though not extensively detailed, the book acknowledges "a relationship structure that can be viewed as a kind of non-hierarchical polyamory" that "doesn't believe in social constructs of relationships in the typical way" A glossary of Polyamory Terms – Polyamory UK
#### Core Principles Across All Models
The book defines an "ethical slut" as someone who "must care for and communicate with their partner or partners, regardless of the degree of commitment in any given relationship" The Ethical Slut - Wikipedia
Key principles emphasized throughout include:

- Open, honest communication
- Informed consent from all parties
- Respect and care for all partners
- Challenging the "starvation economy" mindset about love
- Managing jealousy through self-reflection and communication

### Types of Bonds and Binding Mechanisms in "The Ethical Slut"
#### 1. Fluid Bonding
Fluid bonding is one of the most specific types of intimate bonding discussed. It involves "not using barriers for sexual activities" and represents a conscious decision to share bodily fluids, typically meaning unprotected sexual intercourse. This creates "a mutual decision to let go of protective layers, affirming that your relationship has reached a point where both partners feel secure enough to engage in a more profound level of intimacy." The Monogamy ExperimentGoogle Books
Fluid bonding is characterized by "mutual trust," "shared vulnerability," and "adaptive safety measures" where partners "regularly review and update safety practices" as they remove physical barriers. Fluid Bonding - The Monogamy Experiment - Ethical Non-Monogamy | Polyamory | Open Relationships
#### 2. Emotional Bonds and Commitment Levels
The book recognizes multiple levels of emotional commitment:
Primary/Anchor Partnerships:
An "anchor partner" can be defined in two ways: "a partner you are practically or logistically enmeshed with, such as a person you are living with, married to, or have children with" or "someone who emotionally grounds you and is someone you rely on for long-term partnership." BustleMichelle Hy
Secondary and Tertiary Relationships:
Hierarchical relationships use "primary, secondary, and sometimes tertiary to describe various levels of importance and commitment" which can be either "prescriptive" (predetermined hierarchy) or "descriptive" (reflecting existing entanglements). The Meaning Behind 13 Common Poly Relationship Terms
#### 3. Types of Commitment and Binding
Nesting Partnerships:
A "nesting partner" is specifically "a live-in partner (or partners)" who "may or may not be a primary partner as well, but 'nesting partner' is often used to replace the term primary partner, while still describing a higher level of entanglement, in order to avoid hierarchical language." The Meaning Behind 13 Common Poly Relationship Terms
Logistical vs. Emotional Bonds:
The distinction is made between partners you have "logistical ties with, most likely live with" versus those with whom you have "the deepest or longest term emotional ties." I Love Dating People Who Have Nesting Partners, But Sometimes It’s A Bummer — Michelle Hy | Polyamorous While Asian
#### 4. Sexual and Physical Bonding Types
Body Fluid Monogamy:
While not extensively detailed in "The Ethical Slut" itself, related literature mentions "body fluid monogamy" - limiting fluid exchange to specific partners while maintaining other types of sexual relationships.
Physical Intimacy Bonds:
The book discusses various forms of physical bonding including "non-sexual physical touch—like hugs, cuddles, or simply holding hands—as a continuous reminder of your bond" and "symbolic gestures" like "matching bracelets or shared art." Fluid Bonding - The Monogamy Experiment - Ethical Non-Monogamy | Polyamory | Open Relationships
5. Communication and Agreement-Based Bonds
Relationship Agreements:
The book emphasizes "agreements" over "rules," where "agreements provide flexibility and understanding, allowing for clearer communication between partners" and can include "discussing boundaries, transitioning from one relationship style to another, and deciding how to manage shared resources." Summary of 'The Ethical Slut' by Dossie Easton and Catherine A. Liszt
Consent-Based Bonding:
The book stresses that bonding must be based on "every consensual intimate relationship" and that "any erotic pathway, consciously chosen and mindfully followed, can be a positive, creative force." Random HouseAmazon
6. Spiritual and Philosophical Bonds
Abundance Philosophy:
The book promotes an "abundance" mindset where "when you give love and sex to two or more people instead of to one, you're not dividing a finite resource between them – you're multiplying it" and that "sluts often find that the more love and sex they give away, the more they have." BlinkistRandom House
Ethical Framework Bonds:
Relationships are bound by shared ethical commitments where partners "share their sexuality the way philanthropists share their money: because they have a lot of it to share, because it makes them happy to share it, because sharing makes the world a better place." Random HouseAmazon
7. Boundary-Based Bonding
Safer Sex Protocols:
The book discusses various "safer sex practices" as a form of bonding that creates trust and safety within relationships. The Ethical Slut, Third Edition: A Practical Guide to Polyamory, Open ... - Janet W. Hardy, Dossie Easton - Google Books
Communication Rituals:
Bonding can be maintained through "morning and evening rituals" where partners "start and end your day by sharing your thoughts and feelings, creating a routine that reinforces your emotional closeness." Fluid Bonding - The Monogamy Experiment - Ethical Non-Monogamy | Polyamory | Open Relationships
Core Principle
Throughout all these bonding types, "The Ethical Slut" emphasizes that authentic bonding requires moving away from thinking of "intimacy and love as scarce resources in a 'starvation economy'" and instead embracing the idea that love and connection can be abundant and shared. The Ethical Slut - Wikipedia
The book presents bonding not as ownership or possession, but as conscious, ethical choices to create specific types of intimate connections with different people based on mutual consent, communication, and care.
